# Noridoc: setup

Path: @/ui/pages/setup

### Overview

- The Setup station (ENG-243): session creation (PRD v2 §8.1, redesign §6.1). A **facilitator** surface (§7.2 — denser text/counters allowed). Picks a bucket audio (§7.4, with the collection-consent indicator §12/O6), names the story (falls back to the filename without extension), and confirms pipeline-use consent. The granularity **level** is **displayed, not chosen** (ENG-352): it belongs to the project and is confirmed on @/ui/pages/settings. There is no numeric bead-seconds field either (§6.1/§8.1).
- On create: `GranularityResolver.resolve(level, acousteme)` → beadSec, `BucketSource.fetchBytes` → `AudioEngine.decode` → `buildBeads` (grid) + `hashPCM` (manifest_id) client-side → `SessionStore.create` → persist the initial DTO (`toSessionDto` via `autosave`+`flush`, so the session is resumable/exportable) → `sessionStore.load(state)` (the live domain session) → `navigate('/session/:id')`. The shell then renders **Escuta 1** because a fresh session is `mode='escuta'` + `whole.confirmed=false`.
- `Setup({ bucket, resolver, audioEngine, store, projectSettings, canEdit, projectId, navigate })` — ports arrive **by prop** in tests; in production they resolve through the mode-aware defaults in `ports.ts` (ENG-247). `projectId` is optional: without the prop, the create handler resolves it on the spot via `defaultProjectId()`. `navigate` is injectable so tests observe the async navigation without touching history.
- **The granularity lock** (ENG-363, `granularity-lock.tsx`). A project with no confirmed bead size puts the whole station behind a **blocking Radix dialog**: it does not dismiss on Esc or on a click outside, because there is no decision to take here other than going to take it. Two variants, chosen by `canEdit`: a project admin gets "Definir o tamanho da conta" → `/settings` plus a quiet way back to the dashboard; anyone else is told whom to ask and gets the dashboard exit — **no disabled control**, since a dead button offers a decision the person cannot make (§9.5). The role is only read when the level is missing, and the dialog waits for the answer (`canConfirm === null`) instead of opening as "ask an admin" and swapping the text under an admin's eyes. The prototype the visuals come from is `docs/design/trava-granularidade.html`.
- **Two refusals guard the grid** (ENG-352). A project with no level yet does not fall back to a default — it is stopped by the lock above, because a default would elect the corpus's coordinate system by omission (`preflight` still refuses underneath, as defence in depth). And an audio whose acousteme resolves to a `bead_sec` different from the project's stamped one is refused outright: normalizing it to the stored value would break the O8 rule, and cutting it would split the project across two grids. The comparison is at **millisecond** resolution, which is the precision `manifest_id` keeps (`round(beadSec × 1000)`) — comparing raw floats would fail identical audios on rounding noise.
- **"Até onde vamos hoje?"** (ENG-653, v4 prototype lines 343-349): a card in the narrow column where the facilitator picks how far the pair mean to get today — six fixed chips (`2 cenas`, `4 cenas`, `12 conversas`, `fechar a Triagem`, `fechar as Frases`, `a história toda`), no free text and no numeric input. Picking the chosen one clears it; no goal is a valid state, and then the top bar simply has no mark. The eyebrow says "só a facilitadora", which is also why the counts in those labels are allowed here: the mark and the celebration that come out of the pick are seen by both and carry no numbers (§9.2). The pick goes to @/ui/state/goal-store.ts, **never into the session DTO** — see that folder's doc for why. The chips are the @/ui/atoms `Chip` (its `selected` prop is what gives them `aria-pressed`) recoloured by a context-scoped rule in `setup.css`, not a new atom variant. The note under the chips reads "A meta é conforto, não regra." and stops there: ENG-653 shipped it with a first sentence promising the goal could be changed midway, and ENG-669 removed that half on the owner's call — the chips exist only here, @/ui/app/router.tsx has no per-station route back to Setup, and the session mode is in-memory domain state, so there is no surface on which that promise could be kept. The v4 prototype (line 348) still shows both sentences — this is a deliberate divergence, not drift.
- Pass a **per-test** `FixtureProjectSettings` rather than letting the default resolve: the app-global singleton remembers the stamped grid, so one test would leak the divergence guard into the next.

### How it fits into the larger codebase

- **Routed station** (ENG-272). The shell (@/ui/app/App.tsx) matches `/setup` and resolves the `setup` station, so the dashboard's "Nova sessão" reaches it. The station registry auto-discovers `ui/pages/setup/index.tsx` by glob; the page is otherwise self-contained and tested directly.
- **Radix radio-group** (`@radix-ui/react-radio-group`) drives the audio picker — roving focus + ARIA for free. It no longer drives granularity level cards (since ENG-352 the level is the project's and this page only displays it — @/ui/pages/settings holds that radio group), nor the three entry doors, which are gone: ENG-311 kept _Confirmar uma entrega_ and _Retomar um retorno_ visible-but-disabled to announce the path, and the owner has since removed the selector entirely — two of three options opened nothing, and a chooser with one option chooses nothing. Loading a delivery or a saved return remains reachable as the `/imports` station (ENG-248); when those paths work, they come back as their own entry, not as disabled cards here. Note `docs/PRD-redesign.md` §7.1 still describes the three doors as radio cards.
- **Reference faithfulness (docs/reference/index.html `segment()`).** slug = `title.trim() || filename-without-extension || 'colar'`. The pinned copy — the §O7 trust line, the bead-lock note (meaning preserved without the removed numeric field), the decode-failure and no-audio messages — is unchanged in PT but now lives as dictionary values under the `setup` namespace in @/ui/i18n/pt.ts (with EN in `en.ts`), not as constants exported from this page. Tests assert against `pt.setup.*` so the pinned wording is still checked byte-for-byte.
- **Boundaries.** `try/catch` wraps ONLY the `AudioEngine.decode` call (the typed `AudioDecodeError` boundary); grid/hash/`createSession` are pure and `SessionStore` IO bubbles (CLAUDE.md). Consent gate: an unchecked pipeline-consent checkbox blocks creation, with the guidance line resolved from the dictionary. The decode error interpolates the engine's raw `detail` message into the translated frame — the detail itself is whatever the audio engine threw and is not translated.
- **`ports.ts`** — the default ports are **mode-aware** (ENG-247, keyed off `API_MODE` from @/ui/app/api-config.ts): `defaultBucket()` returns the app-global `appBucket()` (@/ui/app/bucket-adapter.ts — real HTTP bucket or the synthetic fixture); `defaultAudioEngine()` picks the real `WebAudioEngine` (decodes the pilot's mp3/wav so hash/grid come from true PCM) or the `FixtureAudioEngine`; `defaultSessionStore()` returns the app-global `appSessionStore()` (@/ui/app/session-adapter.ts, ENG-272), so a session created here appears in the Dashboard list and is readable by Export; `defaultProjectId()` resolves the owning project — in real mode via `resolveProjectId()` (`my-project-roles`, the SAME cache the bucket uses, so the project whose audios the Setup lists is the project the session is created under), in fixture the usual synthetic id; `defaultCanEdit()` reads the role from the same cache (fixture: always true) and **@/ui/pages/settings re-exports it from here**, so the lock's variant and what the Settings screen offers cannot drift apart. The granularity resolver stays a module singleton. The defaults are production-only and intentionally untested (tests inject the ports).

### Things to Know

- **Two columns of the same height, and the create button never moves** (ENG-386; design source: item 1 of `docs/design/pacote-melhorias-ui.html` + `.md` — a later owner delivery written for this screen, so under precedence rule 2 it wins over the older Protótipo for the _arrangement_; the visual language, cards and watermark still come from the Protótipo). With a large delivery the audio list used to grow until "Criar a sessão" sat below the fold, so a facilitator who had just picked an audio could not see the one action that moves the session forward.
  - The fix is the arrangement, not a ceiling. `.cds-setup` gets a **definite height**, `calc(100dvh - 64px)`; 64px is the shell header, the only chrome above this station (the fio-de-contas stepper exists only inside a session). That definite height is what `flex: 1 1 auto` on `.cds-setup-form` → `.cds-setup-cols` has to draw from. Left column `.cds-setup-col-list` (`flex: 1.3`) holds the scrolling window; right column `.cds-setup-col-side` (`max-width: 380px`) holds the granularity, the selection summary, the title field, consent, and `.cds-setup-foot` — pinned to the column's foot with `margin-top: auto`.
  - **`min-height: 0` is banned from that chain, and the ban is load-bearing.** It is the reflex fix for a flex child that will not shrink, and here it causes a worse bug than the original: the column becomes free to shrink below its own scroll window, the window then spills over the action column, and **the button stays on screen the whole time** — so the screen is unreadable while every size assertion still passes. It is also unnecessary: the list window is a scroll container, and a flex item that scrolls already has an automatic minimum of zero. With the links intact, the chain's minimum stays "what actually has to fit", and below that the station's `overflow: auto` takes over. `setup.test.tsx` asserts the absence; `setup.browser.test.tsx` measures the consequence (`listaInvadeAAcao`), and that assertion was checked against the broken CSS — it reports 29,659px² of overlap there and 0 here.
  - The list window has **no `max-height`**. An earlier attempt on this issue capped it at `min(30vh, 280px)`, and that was wrong: a pixel ceiling only guarantees the button on tall windows. `flex: 1 1 0` makes the list the one thing that yields height, at any viewport size. Under 900px wide the columns stack, and the button still does not sink — the stacked columns are _still_ inside a definite height.
  - **The selection summary moved next to the action.** "Áudio pronto" used to render inside the selected row; it is now `SelectedAudio` in the right column, so the confirmation lands where the eyes already are. The row itself is still marked by Radix's `data-state="checked"`.
  - Nothing in the chain down to the button may use `overflow: hidden` — the station is `overflow: auto` purely as a safeguard for very short viewports, and the corner watermark clips itself in a decorative `inset: 0` layer so that safeguard cannot raise a phantom scrollbar.
  - **The watermark's bottom offset is load-bearing for the same reason the button is.** This station's bottom-right corner is where "Criar a sessão" lives, so the mark is pushed far enough down that it passes _below_ the button rather than behind it. Pulling it back up puts a pale block under the one action that moves the session forward. It is `pointer-events: none`, so it never stole the click — this is about the eye, not the mouse.
  - The button stays **always clickable and validates on click** (`preflight` sets the inline guidance): §9.5 guides, it does not punish, so the delivery's `aria-disabled` variant is deliberately not adopted.
  - **Where it is proven:** `setup.browser.test.tsx` (real Chromium, the `browser` vitest project — jsdom does no layout, so the condition that matters cannot be asserted in `dom`) measures that the button is fully on screen and the page needs no scrolling with a 40-audio delivery, and that growing the delivery from 8 to 60 does not move the button by a pixel. **It must import `ui/tokens/base.css` and `tokens.css`**, exactly what `ui/app/main.tsx` loads: without `base.css` there is no `box-sizing: border-box`, the station's padding adds to its height, and the measurement describes a screen that does not exist. In the `dom` project, `setup.test.tsx` guards the two things that would silently undo this — that the 64px discount still matches the height declared in `ui/app/header.css`, and that no link of the flex chain has re-acquired `min-height: 0`.
  - **Known floor:** the layout holds down to a **620px-tall** viewport at widths of 1024px and up, and down to **732px** once the columns stack (below 900px wide) — stacking needs more vertical room, which is why the stacked floor is the higher of the two. Below those heights the station's `overflow: auto` safeguard takes over and the button needs scrolling. Measured in Chromium by scanning heights in 4px steps, not estimated.
- **Visual language from the Protótipo** (`docs/design/Colar de Sons - Protótipo.dc.html`): the frame — eyebrow, title, footer — keeps the centered 640px column (`.cds-setup > *`), an eyebrow ("Preparação", `setup.eyebrow`) above the title, a large `ShemaIcon` watermark in the corner, and white cards (radius 15px, `--cds-shadow-card`) for the audio picker.
- **The footer is one line, and it is a disclosure, not decoration** (`setup.disclosure`). It used to be two notes — the §5 trust line and the AI-voice notice — and the owner cut it to one: the screen carried too much text. What did NOT shrink is the disclosure itself. PRD §4 counts "disclosed on the setup screen" among the four conditions that make the synthetic question voice acceptable at all, §8 asks for it beside the trust line, and the TTS provider's policy requires the same. The single line still says the audio stays in the project, that the guide's voice is synthetic, and that the questions are written by people. It does not name the other two carve-outs (machine transcription, machine translation) — it never did, and §8 asks for all three.
